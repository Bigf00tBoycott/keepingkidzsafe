SMALL STEPS YOU CAN TAKE TOGETHER

Make a plan.
Make it yours.

Use these conversation guides as starting points. Adjust them to your family’s ages, needs and routines.

01 / CONNECTION

Your family check-in

  1. Make room. Pick a time when you can listen without rushing.
  2. Ask an open question. “What felt good this week? What felt confusing or uncomfortable?”
  3. Name support. Identify trusted adults your child can speak with.
  4. Choose one next step. Agree on something to talk about or review together.
02 / DIGITAL LIFE

A shared device agreement

  1. Talk about use. Which apps, games and conversations matter to your child?
  2. Review privacy. Look together at profile visibility and messaging settings.
  3. Agree on routines. Discuss downloads, spending and when devices fit into family life.
  4. Keep a help route open. Agree on what to do when a message or interaction feels wrong.

03 / OUT & ABOUT

Before you head out

  1. Set the plan. Discuss where you are going and who is supervising.
  2. Agree on check-ins. Decide how to communicate as appropriate for your child.
  3. Plan for a change. Discuss what to do if you are separated or delayed.
  4. Review afterward. Talk about what worked and what you would change.
04 / EVERYDAY SAFETY

Choose one routine to review

Start with a real part of your week: a car journey, time near water, outdoor play or a room in your home. Use guidance relevant to your child’s age rather than a one-size-fits-all list.
