01 / DIGITAL LIFE

Online & connected

Talk about who can see posts, who can send messages, and what information stays private. Review settings together as apps and needs change.

Start a conversation

“What do you like about the apps or games you use? What would you do if a message made you uncomfortable?”

02 / DAILY ROUTINES

Home & everyday

Use age-appropriate safety information to review the places children spend time. Everyday routines can include travel, play, water and home environments.

Start a conversation

“Which parts of our daily routine could we plan more carefully? What questions should we look up together?”

Browse Safe Kids Worldwide safety topics →
03 / REAL-WORLD CONFIDENCE

Out in the world

Before an outing, agree on who is supervising, how to stay in touch, and what to do if plans change. Adapt the conversation to your child’s age and abilities.

Start a conversation

“If we get separated or a plan changes, who could you ask for help? How will we find each other?”

Build an outing plan together →
04 / TRUST & COMMUNICATION

Keep the door open

Make room for children to describe worries in their own words. Ask open questions and remind them they can tell a trusted adult when they need help.
