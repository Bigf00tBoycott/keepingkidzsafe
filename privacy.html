Privacy & use

A simple explanation of how this educational resource works.

No reports or accounts

This website has no account registration or incident-reporting forms. Use the official organizations linked on the Get Help page to make a report.

Your checklist

The homepage checklist works in the current page. Its selections are not saved by the website’s own code and reset when you reload or leave.

Hosting and external services

The hosting provider may process connection information such as IP addresses and request logs, and may use security cookies. External links take you to organizations with their own privacy practices. This site’s authored pages do not embed third-party advertising or analytics; hosting settings can add services separately.

Educational information

These resources are starting points for family conversations, not individualized professional advice or emergency services. Links to organizations do not imply affiliation or endorsement.
