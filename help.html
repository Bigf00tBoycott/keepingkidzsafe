IMMEDIATE DANGER

Contact emergency services.

If a child is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services. In the United States, call 911.

Suspected child sexual exploitation

Use NCMEC’s CyberTipline information and official reporting route. Reports are made directly to NCMEC, not through this website.
